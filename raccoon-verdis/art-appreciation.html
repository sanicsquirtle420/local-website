More Kiriko!

Kiriko's background is a beautiful study in contrast. Raised in Kanezaka, she was trained by two of the most influential figures in her life: her mother, Asa, a master swordsman who also trained Genji and Hanzo Shimada, and her grandmother, who taught her the spiritual ways of the Kitsune fox spirit. This upbringing granted Kiriko a lethal proficiency with blades alongside a profound connection to the supernatural. For years, the Shimada clan maintained order in the region, but their eventual downfall left a power vacuum quickly filled by the ruthless Hashimoto clan. The Hashimoto terrorized Kanezaka, demanding extortion money and taking Kiriko's father hostage to force him to forge weapons. Refusing to watch her home crumble, Kiriko formed the "Yōkai"—a group of young, tech-savvy rebels dedicated to disrupting Hashimoto operations through targeted guerrilla tactics. Kiriko acts as the bridge between old-world tradition and new-world rebellion, fighting not for global politics, but for the safety of her community. (AI Generated Text)

More Juno!

Juno's story begins not on Earth, but in the harsh, crimson deserts of Mars. She was born and raised on the Lucis Colony, a research outpost established by Lucis Interstellar. The project aimed to terraform Mars and find sustainable alternatives for humanity's survival. Juno's parents were lead scientists on this mission, dedicating their lives to making the Red Planet habitable. Her mother, Dr. Jiayi Space, was a brilliant engineer, and her father was an expert in atmospheric sciences.
Growing up as the first generation born on Mars, Juno's childhood was vastly different from anyone on Earth. Her world was one of airtight seals, sterile corridors, and the constant hum of life-support systems. Despite the isolation, she possessed a naturally cheerful, deeply empathetic, and curious disposition. Her parents trained her rigorously in aerospace engineering, pilot mechanics, and medical technology, preparing her to one day inherit the mantle of Martian colonization. (AI Generated Text)
